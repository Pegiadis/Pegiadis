AI engineer building products that ship to production. Based in Thessaloniki, Greece.

Background in Supply Chain, Business Analytics, and AI. Three degrees that taught me the operations, data flows, and revenue models behind what I build.

## What I work on

- **AI applications.** Production RAG pipelines, tool-calling agents with guardrails, document understanding (OCR to structured data), and LLM features shipped with evals and observability — not demos.
- **Full-stack products.** End-to-end SaaS platforms from database design to deployment. Multi-tenant systems, real-time features, and architecture that scales.
- **Data and ML pipelines.** Large-scale data processing for AI and analytics. ETL, geospatial pipelines, and automated ingestion from satellite imagery to API aggregation.

## Selected client work

- **Production RAG platform** for a major Greek food manufacturer — OCR document ingestion, hybrid retrieval with reranking, multi-model orchestration (OpenAI primary, Claude fallback), per-tenant permission filtering, and streaming answers. Label-compliance checks that took hours now take minutes.
- **Natural-language analytics agent** for an environmental-tech company — a read-only SQL tool-calling agent over PostGIS surveillance data, with AST-level query validation, Langfuse tracing, and Prometheus metrics. From question to chart in seconds; live in production.
- **AI claim-approval system** for a Greek auto-glass company — insurance claims arrive via a partner API, Claude Vision reads the documents, per-insurer completeness checks run across 24 Greek insurers, and approval emails are drafted with human review in the loop.
